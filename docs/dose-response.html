Frozen-FM-Eval — Dose–response

Whenever you would report a rank, find the dose that produces it, put the criticized method class on the same curve, and report the mechanism.

D.vocabDOSE

Vocabulary dose

Sweep the fraction of readable genes across atlases, and causally inside a fixed atlas by renaming genes so the tokenizer cannot read them. An apparent cross-species FM failure becomes a tokenization cliff to chance once almost no genes are readable; otherwise at parity.

  • Vocabulary-coverage sweep across atlases
  • Causal in-atlas ablation (unreadable genes)
  • scGPT vocabulary ablation

D.batchDOSE

Batch-shift dose

Score coverage and ECE degradation against measured shift strength (held-out-batch discriminability). A calibration collapse attributed to FMs afflicts classical baselines by the same amount: it is a property of the regime.

  • Coverage and ECE versus measured batch-shift strength
  • Frozen-FM probe under shift
  • Intrinsic held-out-batch discriminability

Batch-shift outputs on Zenodo

D.spatialDOSE

Spatial-aggregation dose

Smooth per-cell representations over k spatial neighbours. A spatial-FM gap closes once a per-cell baseline is given the one operation it lacked. This cell is the protocol mechanism, not finding 3.

  • Spatial-neighbour aggregation dose
  • Fold-blocked spatial-aggregation dose
