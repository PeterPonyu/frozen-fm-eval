Frozen-FM-Eval — Protocol

P.iMOVE

Pair every agreement metric with a circularity-free re-check

Compute a non-linear (kNN) probe of the same labels and a reference-free structure metric (R²expr). If the ground-truth labels explain less structure than the clustering they grade, the agreement metric is scoring a coarser cut than the data supports.

  • fair_recheck.py
  • parity_probe_types.py
  • direct_circularity.py
  • r2_null_control.py
  • circularity_hvg_robustness.py

P.iiMOVE

Treat tokenizer/vocabulary coverage as a measured variable

Record, per atlas × model, the fraction of features that map into the model's vocabulary and the expression mass they carry. Report every quality number against coverage.

  • vocab_dose_response.py

P.iiiMOVE

Argue parity by equivalence (TOST ±0.02 AUROC), not by an absent p-value

A non-significant test is not evidence of equivalence. Use a two-one-sided-test against a pre-declared margin. A boundary result is reported as no significant difference, not as a clean parity pass.

  • parity_pooled_cluster_ci.py
  • parity_robustness.py

P.ivMOVE

Score the reliability axis explicitly

Report split-conformal coverage under the deployment shift, calibration (ECE), and selective abstention (risk–coverage, AURC), including fairness to rare classes.

  • scatac_audit.py
  • scatac_fm_matched.py
  • fm_all_audit.py
  • scatac_batch_shift.py
