Frozen-FM-Eval — Findings

F1FINDING

Vocabulary-matched atlases: frozen scFMs neither beat nor trail simple baselines (PCA/HVG/scVI)

On vocabulary-matched atlases, frozen scFMs neither beat nor trail simple baselines. The mapped mechanism is vocabulary coverage as a measured variable, not a rank table.

Evidence on the vocabulary dose page

F2FINDING

Cross-batch calibration collapse is a general exchangeability failure, not an FM defect (20/24 atlases; scATAC is flat)

Coverage and ECE degrade under batch shift for classical baselines as well. The mapped mechanism is batch-shift dose, not an FM-only defect.

Evidence on the batch-shift dose page

Who wins is set by task and metric, not scale or architecture (Geneformer 104M→316M buys nothing)
