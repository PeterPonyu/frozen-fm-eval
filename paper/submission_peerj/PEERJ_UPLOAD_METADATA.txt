File type: Figure
Title: Spatial-smoothing dose response for the residual niche-prediction gap.
Legend: Per-cell PCA and foundation-model representations are progressively smoothed over spatial neighbours and evaluated with spatially blocked cross-validation. 10. Figure 8

Legend: Foundation models obtain greater batch mixing at the cost of increased cell-type blurring across three atlases. The purpose-built scVI model provides a stronger mixing-purity trade-off, showing that the apparent foundation-model integration advantage is not cost-free.

13. Figure 11
File type: Figure
Title: Observational and causal tests of the gene-vocabulary coverage artifact.
Legend: Panel A relates tokenizer coverage to zero-shot classification across atlases. Panel B removes readable genes within fixed atlases and shows stable performance until coverage falls below roughly 1,000–2,000 genes, followed by chance performance at zero readable genes.

14. Figure 12
File type: Figure
Title: Cross-batch calibration instability of frozen scGPT embeddings.
Legend: Across four scRNA atlases, conformal coverage degrades out of batch even when discrimination transfers. Increasing readout capacity worsens expected calibration error, while temperature scaling closes only part of the calibration gap.
Filename: Figure12.pdf

15. Figure 13
File type: Figure
Title: Selective abstention and temperature scaling across 24 scRNA atlases.
Legend: Panel A shows that selective abstention improves accuracy at 80% coverage for every baseline. Panel B shows that temperature scaling reduces cross-batch expected calibration error in most atlases but does not eliminate the coverage failure.
Filename: Figure13.pdf

16. Figure 14
File type: Figure
Title: Pervasive conformal coverage collapse for simple baselines under scRNA batch shift.
Legend: Cross-batch conformal coverage gaps are shown for simple baselines across 24 leakage-controlled scRNA atlases. Positive gaps in 20 of 24 atlases indicate a general exchangeability failure rather than a defect specific to foundation models.
Filename: Figure14.pdf
